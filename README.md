## 📌 Project Overview
The official website of an academic department is its digital frontline. This project aims to reimagine and reconstruct the web presence of the Computer Science and Engineering Department at Veer Surendra Sai University of Technology (VSSUT), Burla. 

Built by the students, for the students and faculty, this platform focuses on intuitive navigation, modern aesthetics, fast load times, and accessibility—bringing our digital infrastructure on par with premier engineering institutes.

## ✨ Key Features
* **Dynamic Notice Board:** Real-time updates for academic notices, exam schedules, and departmental announcements.
* **Faculty & Research Portfolios:** Detailed, easily navigable profiles highlighting faculty research areas, publications, and contact information.
* **Academic Resources:** Centralized hubs for syllabi, academic calendars, timetables, and lab manuals.
* **Student & Alumni Showcase:** A dedicated section celebrating student achievements, hackathon wins, and our robust alumni network.
* **Responsive UI/UX:** A mobile-first, highly accessible design inspired by the clean, data-rich interfaces of top-tier universities.
