Overview

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at VSSUT (formerly UCE Burla) stands as a premier beacon of technical education in Eastern India. Since its inception, we have been committed to producing elite engineers and researchers.

Our comprehensive curriculum bridges theoretical foundations with modern industrial practices, empowering students to tackle the complex computational challenges of tomorrow while maintaining a strong ethos of ethics and societal impact.
